# National Research Software Day 2027

<img src="/img/nrsd2027.jpg" alt="banner" style="width:100%" class="rounded-xl "/>

**The National Research Software Day is coming to Utrecht in April 2027!**

NL-RSE is excited to announce the next edition of the National Research Software Day (NRSD), taking place in **Utrecht in April 2027**.

This edition will be hosted by the [Research Data Managers and Engineers](https://www.uu.nl/en/research/research-data-management) (RDM team) at Utrecht University, in collaboration with the [eScience Centre](https://www.esciencecenter.nl) and NL-RSE.

As in previous editions, NRSD 2027 will bring together people from across the Dutch research software community to share experiences, exchange ideas, and learn from one another. The program will be community-led, with a call for abstracts opening later this year.

More information about the exact date, program, registration, and call for abstracts will be announced as plans take shape. Watch this space for updates!
