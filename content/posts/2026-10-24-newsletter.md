Welcome to the October 2026 NL-RSE Community Newsletter! As autumn gets underway, there is plenty happening across the Dutch research software community. First, a date for your calendar: National Research Software Day returns on 20 April 2027, hosted by Utrecht University! We’ll be sharing more details soon, including a call for volunteers to join the organising committee. Interested in helping shape the event? Read on to find out how you can get involved!

## Dates for your calendar

**djehuty Community Hour**   
**28 October @ online**   
The djehuty project, 4TU.ResearchData's open-source research data repository platform, has launched a monthly Community Hour. Whether you're a developer or simply interested in the project, this is an opportunity to hear about ongoing work, share feedback and get involved through code contributions, bug reports or ideas. External contributions are very welcome! The first session takes place online on 28 October at 15:00 CET. Find out more and join via the <a href="https://djehuty.4tu.nl/community/community-hour/" style="color: #650045; font-weight: bold; font-style: italic;">djehuty Community Hour page</a>.   

**Deadline: deRSE27 call for contributions**   
**31 October**   
The deadline for submissions to deRSE27, the 7th Conference for Research Software Engineering in Germany, is approaching. The conference welcomes contributions from across the research software engineering community, including talks, posters, demos, tutorials, workshops, meet-ups and hackathons. The event will take place from 23–25 February 2027 at TU Dortmund, Germany, with a focus on research software engineering in the age of generative and agentic AI, research on research software engineering, and research software security. Find submission guidelines and further details on the <a href="https://events.hifis.net/event/4318/" style="color: #650045; font-weight: bold; font-style: italic;">deRSE27 event page</a>.   

**RST-NL meetup: Open Source Alternatives & Carpentries Community Talk**   
**19 November @ Amsterdam**   
Join Research Software Training NL for an afternoon of collaboration, learning and networking for research software trainers and support staff. The programme includes a co-working sprint to develop a comparative list of open-source alternatives to proprietary software, a Carpentries Community Talk by Toby Hodges on curriculum developments in HPC and AI, and networking drinks. Places are limited, and you can register for individual parts of the programme via the <a href="https://researchsoftwaretraining.nl/november-19-open-source-software-alternatives-carpentries-curriculum-developments-hpc-ai-networking-drinks/" style="color: #650045; font-weight: bold; font-style: italic;">event page</a>.   

**ODISSEI 10th anniversary conference**   
**24 November @ Utrecht**   
What will the next decade of computational social science bring? Join us at ODISSEI’s 10th anniversary conference on 24 November 2026 at Jaarbeurs, Utrecht, to explore new research, connect with colleagues across disciplines, and help shape what comes next. With keynote speakers Arnout van de Rijt (European University Institute) and Ana-Andreea Stoica (Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems), we’ll celebrate ten years of ODISSEI and look ahead to new possibilities for social science research. Registration is free and now open via the <a href="https://odissei-data.nl/event/odissei-conference-2026/" style="color: #650045; font-weight: bold; font-style: italic;">conference website</a>.   

**Galaxy hackathon in the Dutch Life Science and Health**   
**11 December @ Utrecht**   
Are you developing software for research in Life Science and Health? Would you like to package your software for [Galaxy](https://galaxyproject.org)? The FAIR Tool Framework is organising a hackathon on December 11 at SURF Amsterdam. Register your interest via <a href="https://forms.gle/Z6cG5zkqQk7E6KqJ9" style="color: #650045; font-weight: bold; font-style: italic;">this form</a>.   

## News

**Call for volunteers: National Research Software Day 2027**   
**20 April @ Utrecht**   
The next National Research Software Day will take place on 20 April 2027 with Utrecht University as the host. As in previous editions, the programme will be shaped by the community, with a call for submissions opening at the end of November. But there’s another way to get involved: we’re looking for community members to join the conference organising committee! This is a great opportunity to help shape the event and get to know and work with a diverse team of colleagues from the eScience Centre, Utrecht University, TU Delft and more. The call for committee members will open in early November, but you can already register your interest by emailing _info@nl-rse.org_. Keep an eye on the <a href="https://forms.gle/Z6cG5zkqQk7E6KqJ9" style="color: #650045; font-weight: bold; font-style: italic;">NRSD 2027 page</a> for updates.   

**OSCNL Mobility and Development Call**   
Open Science NL's Mobility and Development Programme offers an opportunity for research supporters, including research software engineers, software stewards and trainers, to visit an institute abroad, learn from colleagues and share their experiences back at their home organisation. Applications remain open until 3 December 2026. Find out more <a href="https://www.openscience.nl/en/calls/mobility-and-development-programme" style="color: #650045; font-weight: bold; font-style: italic;">here</a>.   

**NVIDIA joins European Commission initiative to support AI in science**   
NVIDIA has joined the European Commission’s AI in Science Enablers Initiative. Its pledge includes access to computing resources, scientific AI models and software, [open training materials](https://www.nvidia.com/en-eu/training/open-source/), and technical expertise to help researchers advance scientific discovery. Find out more in the <a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/EC-scientific-computing-pledge/" style="color: #650045; font-weight: bold; font-style: italic;">official announcement</a>.   

## Blog posts, tools & more

**Dutch Contributions to IRSC and RSECon26**   
What contributions did RSEs from the Netherlands bring to the international research software community? This <a href="https://nl-rse.org/posts/2026-10-07-irsc-rsecon-contributions" style="color: #650045; font-weight: bold; font-style: italic;">NL-RSE blog post</a> highlights Dutch contributions to the International Research Software Conference (IRSC) and Research Software Engineering Conference 2026 (RSECon26), both held in Sheffield in September.   

**Critical vulnerabilities in PHP affect websites worldwide**   
In October, two critical vulnerabilities in PHP triggered emergency patching and precautionary website shutdowns worldwide. In this <a href="https://www.wiz.io/blog/php2shell-php-rce" style="color: #650045; font-weight: bold; font-style: italic;">blog post</a> the Wiz Research team who discovered the vulnerabilities explain how long-standing flaws in PHP could let attackers take control of websites or access sensitive data. They also describe how AI helped them uncover the flaws and how researchers and industry partners coordinated to get fixes in place.   

**endoflife.date: tracking software end-of-life and support**   
Keeping track of software versions and their support lifecycles is an important part of maintaining reliable research software. <a href="https://endoflife.date" style="color: #650045; font-weight: bold; font-style: italic;">endoflife.date</a> aggregates end-of-life and support information for a wide range of software projects, helping users understand when versions are no longer supported and plan upgrades accordingly.   

**AI Declaration Format**   
As generative AI becomes more common in research and software development, communicating how AI tools have been used is increasingly important. The <a href="https://app.ai-declaration.org/generator/" style="color: #650045; font-weight: bold; font-style: italic;">AI Declaration Format</a> provides a machine-readable format for declaring AI use, helping make these declarations more structured and consistent.
